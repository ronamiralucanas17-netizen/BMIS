About BMIS Ormoc City

A next-generation Barangay Management Information System integrating Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and Machine Learning to enhance public service and disaster resilience.

GIS Integration

Visualizing community infrastructure, household locations, and disaster-prone areas using interactive mapping technology.

ML Analytics

Utilizing Machine Learning to predict household vulnerability and identify critical service gaps within the community.

Smart Governance

Streamlining administrative processes, service requests, and resident documentation for a more efficient barangay.

System Overview

The Barangay Management Information System (BMIS) for Ormoc City is designed to empower local officials with data-driven insights while providing residents with easy access to government services.

  • Real-time Incident Reporting & Monitoring
  • Automated Document Issuance & Verification
  • Disaster Risk Assessment & Evacuation Mapping
